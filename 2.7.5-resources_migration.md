A new HUB update is coming with changes to Resources, lesson materials, events, and links.
Resources replaces Tutorials/Information

- The Tutorials/Information area has been renamed Resources.
- Existing PDFs, videos, FAQs, categories, and company assignments will be migrated automatically.
- Existing content will initially appear under Others.
- A resource card can now contain multiple files, links, videos, and FAQs.
- Resource cards can be organised into sections and given PNG or JPEG cover images.
- Admins can create, edit, delete, and drag sections or resource cards to reorder them.
- Organisations only see sections containing resources assigned to them.
- If an organisation has only one available section, it goes directly to the resources inside it.
- Deleting a section moves its resource cards to Others rather than deleting them.
  Please check company visibility carefully when creating or editing resource cards.
  Lesson and event editing
- Admins now have a small Edit button directly inside lesson and event pop-ups.
- This allows admins to move from the teacher-facing preview directly to the lesson editor.
- Writers will continue to use their existing lesson-management screens.
  Monthly Materials
- The course selector has been removed.
- Monthly Materials now collects the relevant lessons across all active plans for the selected organisation.
- Teachers can click a lesson title in Monthly Materials to preview its full lesson and resources.
- The month selector has been improved and should open more consistently.
- Sunday lesson dates have been corrected.
  Lesson material fields
- Lesson materials are now separated into:
  - Basic Materials – standard items normally available at the school.
  - Purchase Required – items that may need to be ordered or specially prepared.
- Monthly Materials displays only the Purchase Required list.
- Evening Classes now support these material fields as well.
- Existing material entries will migrate into Purchase Required.
  Please use Basic Materials for ordinary classroom supplies and Purchase Required only when action or purchasing may be needed.
  Events and Seasonal Activities
- Seasonal Activities remain visible to teachers for up to two months after their event date.
- Party and other Event Activities retain the shorter visibility period.
- Seasonal resource displays have been reorganised to make Kindy, Elementary, and Galaxy English-class files clearer.
  Lesson links
- Lesson resource links are no longer limited to Google Drive and Google Docs.
- Any valid HTTP or HTTPS website can now be linked.
- Links to either HUB access point are converted into internal paths, keeping users on the domain where they are already logged in.
- YouTube, Vimeo, and Google Docs behavior remains supported.
- Invalid URLs and unsupported schemes such as FTP will still be rejected.
  Please contact the development team if migrated content, organisation visibility, lesson materials, or resource files do not look correct after release.
